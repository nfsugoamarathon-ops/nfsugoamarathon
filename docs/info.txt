Registration Process
1. Selects category: 3Km/6Km/ 10Km 
2. Fills full form: Name, DOB, Gender, WhatsApp No, Email, Emergency Contact, Blood Group, T-Shirt Size, Medical Condition
3. Uploads ID Proof (Aadhaar / College ID) if age < 18
4. Pays registration fee online - UPI / Card / Net Banking
5. Gets confirmation SMS + Email with Registration ID within 5 minutes
6. If payment deducted but no confirmation, wait 30 mins and check email. If still not received, contact support on WhatsApp with payment screenshot

Bib Collection
1. Bib collection on 4th and 5th Dec, 4 PM - 8 PM at Ponda - venue to be announced in group
2. Bring Registration ID + ID Proof
3. Bib cannot be collected by another person without authorization letter + copy of ID
4. No bib distribution on race morning after 6 AM

Race Day - 06-Dec 2026
1. Reporting: 5:00- 5:30AM 
2. Warm-up: 6:00 AM
3. Flag-off: 6: 30AM (10K), 6:45 AM (6Km), 6:50 AM (3Km)
4. Cut-off time: 2 hours for all categories 
5. Water stations every 2 KM, Medical desk at Start/Finish + every 3 KM
6. Results will be published on website within 24 hours

* Eligibility
- 3K: Age 60+ and under 14
- 6K: 15-35 and 36-59
- 10K: 15-35 and 36-59
- Age proof will be verified at bib collection
